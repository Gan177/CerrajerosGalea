# Cerrajeros Galea — Product Context

## Product Purpose
Emergency locksmith SPA for Bizkaia (Basque Country, Spain). Primary conversion goal: phone call. Users land here in high-stress moments and need to act in seconds.

## Register
brand

## Users
Homeowners, tenants, and vehicle owners locked out — mostly on mobile, mostly at night, often stressed and anxious. Secondary: businesses researching a trusted local locksmith before an emergency.

## Brand
- Name: Cerrajeros Galea
- Tone: Direct, authoritative, reassuring. No fluff. Every word builds trust.
- Phone: 663 240 075
- Lead technician: Aitor
- Google rating: 4.9 stars
- Service area: Bizkaia — Bilbao metro + surrounding municipalities
- Specialties: Non-destructive opening, armored doors, Ezkurra / SAG cylinders, vehicle opening

## Color System
- Background: #1A1A1A (near-black, warm tint)
- Surface: #1E1E1E
- Accent red: #C1272D — CTAs, headlines, urgency
- Gold: #FFD700 — phone number, 24h badge, star ratings only
- Muted text: oklch(58–65% 0.005 20)

## Typography
Inter (Google Fonts, 400–900). High contrast between heading weights and body.

## Anti-references
- Cheap/generic locksmith sites with clipart
- Neon-heavy dark designs
- SaaS metrics dashboards
- Anything that looks template-generated or AI-slop

## Strategic Principles
1. Phone CTA always accessible with one thumb on mobile.
2. Trust signals (stars, response time, years of service) above the fold.
3. Dark mode is mandatory — primary use context is nighttime emergency.
4. Page speed over decoration.
